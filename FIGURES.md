## Commands and output files

```sh
# Entire chain, IR, centroid diagnostics and tests:
make test PYTHON=.venv/bin/python

# Main figure chain only, rebuilding upstream results:
make figures PYTHON=.venv/bin/python

# Render all figures from existing results:
.venv/bin/python -m src.figures

# Selected figures:
.venv/bin/python -m src.figures fig6_ruptures fig8_melt_areas fig10_raman
.venv/bin/python -m src.figures Supplementary_Figure_6 Supplementary_Figure_11
```

Each figure writes PNG and PDF to `figures/manuscript/`. Numerical JSON sidecars
are generated for figures 6–10, S1–S3, S5 and S6–S11. S1 also writes fitted spectral
arrays to NPZ. Figures 2–4 and S4 have no JSON sidecar. The shared numerical
regression is saved as `results/fig7_interval_regression.json`; S11 also saves
`results/Supplementary_Figure_11.csv`.

S1–S3, S6 and S11 use a 180 mm canvas and 600 dpi PNG exports. S4 uses the
source-defined width at 300 dpi; S5 uses the shared style and 300 dpi; S7–S10 retain the Figure 6 layout and 300 dpi.
Other main figures use their source-defined single/double-column dimensions.
Some exports are cropped to their artists, so final PDF dimensions can differ
from the canvas dimensions. PDF versions retain vector plotting elements.

## Display and interpretation checks

- Figure 6 and S7–S10 share the same five-family analysis. Input channels are
  grey with small circles; the blue mean is a descriptive guide, not the
  statistic fitted by ruptures. Crystallised observations retain red open
  diamonds. Y limits use fitted glass/melt channels only, with a 5% margin;
  out-of-range crystallised points are clipped, as the figure footer states.
- Figure 7 shows one reference at **219 ±4 °C**, labelled `T_f' at 25 K/min`,
  for the hydrous glass received after piston-cylinder synthesis. Its uncertainty is displayed as supplied
  and it does not constrain the regression. Values/provenance are in
  `ANN_FICTIVE_REFERENCE` in `src/config.py`.
- Figure 8 uses the compositional 0.59 NBO/T starting-glass comparison line
  (`ANN_HYDROUS_GLASS_NBO_T`). Raman NBO/T values are calculated using all five band areas
  and the full covariance with shared conversion errors. The compositional NBO/T
  does not enter the fits.
- Figure 9 shifts absolute fit profiles by the fixed nominal intercept
  **239.7332 °C**. Its finite band width at zero pressure is intentional;
  see [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) for the definition and limits.
- Figure 10 contains the five room-temperature Run 1 acquisitions and 22
  Melt-only observations. The glass quadratic and equal-run-weight parallel
  Melt lines are descriptive guides; no line bridges the unsampled Melt
  pressure interval. Panel b uses fitted T2S/Q4 and its local covariance.
- S1 is selected by pressure proximity among non-crystallised states, not by
  fit quality. S2/S3 use all 52 acquisitions labelled Verre or Melt.
- S11 compares direct GP predictions with MYEGA using **Ae=-1.48** and
  **m=34.5 ±1.2**. The shaded interval varies fragility only; the curves are
  scenarios rather than measured viscosities.

S5 reads three selected raw Ctx spectra from sessions 20250220, 20250303 and
20250408. It applies a cubic baseline over 500–520, 800–840 and 1200–1300 cm⁻¹,
then normalises each corrected spectrum to its maximum. Peak markers and
temperature/pressure annotations are retained from the supplied script; they
are not updated by the pressure measurements code. Its JSON records the
input files and processing parameters. Run it alone with `python -m src.manuscript.supplementary_fig_5`
or `python -m src.figures Supplementary_Fig_5`.
